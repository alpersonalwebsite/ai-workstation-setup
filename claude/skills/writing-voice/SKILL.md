This skill carries your writing voice so drafts come out sounding like you. The
always-on basics live in your global `CLAUDE.md`; this skill adds register,
per-format structure, and examples. Replace the bracketed prompts with your own.

## Registers

Most people write in more than one register. Define yours. A common split:

- **Professional** (colleagues, stakeholders): [short and direct? warm bookends?
  how long does an update run?]
- **Personal** (family, friends): [more relaxed, or more expressive and long?
  what does it sound like when it is just you?]

Say plainly which rules are professional-only, so brevity guidance never flattens
a personal message.

## Per-format structure

- **Quick chat / DM:** [the point first, no greeting? emoji ok?]
- **Status update / notification:** [structure, e.g. situation, then specifics,
  then next step; concrete numbers over adjectives]
- **Request:** [one clear ask; how polite vs direct]
- **Closure:** [what is done and verified, plus anything still open]
- **RCA / technical review:** finding or verdict first, then the evidence that
  proves it, then the fix. One finding per block; worst first when there are
  several.
- **PR / commit:** why, then what changed, then reviewer notes. Follow your git
  rules in `CLAUDE.md`.
- **Docs / README:** scannable. Headers, tables, short lists; show an example
  instead of describing one.
